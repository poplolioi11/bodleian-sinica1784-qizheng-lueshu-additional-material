# 奇症略述：新增材料與藏本導讀

Qi zheng lue shu [Brief accounts of remarkable cases]

版本0.1.0。[發行頁](https://github.com/poplolioi11/bodleian-sinica1784-qizheng-lueshu-additional-material/releases/tag/v0.1.0)；製備時尚未取得DOI。

《奇症略述》（Qi zheng lue shu；編者英譯 Brief accounts of remarkable cases）是嘉約翰（John Glasgow Kerr）在廣州博濟醫局整理的病例與院務報告。牛津博德利圖書館 Sinica 1784 藏本含結石、腫瘤、眼病、創傷、牙病及其他病例的文字與圖說，並附徵信錄、收支、施醫統計、會務及出版記事，可供晚清醫學史、醫療傳教、慈善組織、病人敘事與圖像傳播史研究。館目標示1864至1868年；卷內另有1869年序及1873年附錄，這些日期分別保留。本資料初錄60個新增或跨本圖像身分尚未確認的書面，以JSON、TXT、TEI保存來源對應、細字關係及80筆疑讀；另為全藏本91個原印面提供範圍導讀，31個共享內容面連結 Sinica 2021 既有成果，未作逐字移錄或全本異文校勘。圖像不在公開包內。這是AI輔助初錄，未納入獨立讀稿，未經人類全文雙校或專家審定。

Qi zheng lue shu (editorial translation: Brief accounts of remarkable cases), compiled by John Glasgow Kerr at the Medical Missionary Society's Hospital at Canton, brings together historical case records and hospital reports. Bodleian Sinica 1784 includes accounts and captions concerning stones, tumours, eye conditions, injuries, dental conditions and other cases, alongside an annual report, income and expenditure, attendance figures, organizational business and publishing notices. It supports histories of medicine, medical missions, charity, patient narratives and medical illustration. The catalogue dates the work to 1864–1868; the witness also contains a preface dated 1869 and an appendix dated 1873, recorded as distinct events. This dataset provides an AI-assisted initial transcription of 60 additional or cross-witness image-identity-unverified faces in JSON, TXT and TEI, with source links, small-note relationships and 80 uncertainty records. A coverage guide covers all 91 original printed faces and links 31 shared-content faces to the existing Sinica 2021 dataset; no text has been silently transferred and no full witness collation is claimed. Images are excluded. No independent reading is incorporated in this initial version; it has not undergone full human double collation or expert approval.

本版617個文字單位、80筆疑讀／來源問題、15個字形標記；2個原印小註連回主欄。31個共享面是不同藏本之內容與位置對照，不表示逐字相同。源圖、裁片與專用方法不公開。
