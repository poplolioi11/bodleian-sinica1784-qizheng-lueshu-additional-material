# 奇症略述：新增材料與藏本導讀

Qi zheng lue shu [Brief accounts of remarkable cases]

版本0.2.0。引用資訊見CITATION.cff；製備時本版Zenodo DOI尚待登記。

《奇症略述》（Qi zheng lue shu；編者英譯 Brief accounts of remarkable cases）是嘉約翰（John Glasgow Kerr）在廣州博濟醫局整理的病例與院務報告，牛津博德利圖書館 Sinica 1784 館目標示1864至1868年。藏本收結石、腫瘤、眼病、創傷、牙病等病例與圖說，以及徵信錄、收支、施醫統計、會務和出版記事，可供晚清醫學史、醫療傳教、慈善組織、病人敘事與醫學圖像史研究；卷內1869年序與1873年附錄的日期分別保留。本版提供60個原印面的617個文字單位，以JSON、TXT及TEI連結原件位置，另附逐單位變更與細註關係；全藏本91面中的其餘31個共享內容面只連結Sinica 2021既有成果，未作全本逐字異文校勘。文字由AI輔助初錄及獨立原圖讀稿比對後回圖修訂，尚未經人類全文校勘，疑讀已標記。

Qi zheng lue shu (editorial translation: Brief accounts of remarkable cases) is a collection of case records and hospital reports compiled by John Glasgow Kerr at the Medical Missionary Society's Hospital at Canton (Bodleian Sinica 1784; catalogued 1864–1868). It includes records and captions concerning stones, tumours, eye conditions, injuries and dental conditions, alongside annual reports, accounts, attendance figures, organizational business and publishing notices. It supports histories of medicine, medical missions, charity, patient narratives and medical illustration. The witness's preface dated 1869 and appendix dated 1873 are recorded separately. This version provides 617 text units from 60 original printed faces in JSON, TXT and TEI linked to source locations, with unit-level changes and small-note relationships. The remaining 31 shared-content faces in the 91-face witness are linked to the existing Sinica 2021 dataset; no complete word-for-word collation of the witnesses is claimed. AI-assisted initial and independent source readings were compared and revised against the images; the text has not undergone full human collation, and unresolved readings are marked.
